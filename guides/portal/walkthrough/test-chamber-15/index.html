Portal walkthrough · Part 3: Dual-portal segment

Test Chamber 15

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 15 has the most things to collect so far: five cameras and two radios. It is laid out in four rooms. The first is a double-fling entry room. In the second, a pellet must cross two force fields into its receiver. The third has another double fling, an angled-panel pellet game and a backwards conveyor. The final room has a pellet launcher, two side-room buttons and the receiver that lowers the exit lift.

Collectibles here:

  • Cameras (5): on the right wall before the first room; above or beside the first-room exit; above or beside the second-room exit lift; in the third-room fling side room; and above the final-room entrance. †
  • Radio A: on the support for the first room’s extended platform. †
  • Radio B (15B): in the final room, inside the glass enclosure that holds the pellet receiver. †
  • Five cameras (Test Chamber 16 also has five) and two radios: take note of every location listed for gate 6 and gates 2 and 5.
  • Two radios (15A, 15B). Five cameras, tied with Test Chamber 16 for the most in a single chamber. †

Tip: Emancipation grids appear mid-puzzle, not just at the exit, so plan your portal placement before you pass through them.

Walkthrough

  1. You arrive from Test Chamber 14 by chamberlock elevator. The game autosaves when the map testchmb_a_10 loads, and that map holds Chamber 15 only.
  2. In the first room, put the orange portal on the extended panel above the entrance and the blue one on the checkered floor. Fall twice to gain momentum through a force field. †
  3. Take radio A from the support for the first room’s extended platform. †
  4. In the second room, a pellet has to cross two force fields into the receiver. Put portals on the burn mark and on another wall so the pellet flies into the receiver’s side of the room. Then put a portal in front of the pellet and one on the red light above the receiver before the pellet disappears. A camera sits above and to the right of this room’s exit lift. †
  5. In the third room, drop into a side room to start the fling. A camera sits here. †
  6. In the final room, press the buttons in the two side rooms to open two doors. Then route the pellet, from the extended panel to the scorch mark, into the receiver to lower the exit lift. †
  7. For the second radio, press the two buttons, then use portals, two on the ground and one in front of the enclosure, to jump into the glass enclosure around the pellet receiver. †
  8. Ride the exit lift to the chamberlock, then take the elevator to Test Chamber 16. This ride is one-way. †

From Test Chamber 15 the only way on is Test Chamber 16, through the elevator and the grill, once you solve the chamber.

† Only one source reports this.
