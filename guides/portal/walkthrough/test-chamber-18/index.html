Portal walkthrough · Part 3: Dual-portal segment

Test Chamber 18

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Chamber 18 is the last numbered turret chamber. It is a gauntlet in three parts: (1) a fling platform chain, (2) a turret, pellet and moving platform section, and (3) linked flings to the exit. Each part has distinct hazards.

You arrive from Test Chamber 17 by chamberlock elevator, and the map load autosaves (testchmb_a_14, which holds Chamber 18 only).

Collectibles here:

  • Radios 18A, 18B and 18C: the most radios of any single chamber. All are collectible.
  • You can miss both of the 2 cameras: one is next to the window at the turret-room entrance, the other on the wall at the final-room entrance.
  • Three radios and two cameras in all.

Tip: If you missed “Friendly Fire” back in Test Chamber 16, you can still earn it here. This is a turret chamber, and turrets also appear in the escape.

Walkthrough

  1. Stand on the entry shelf. It overlooks platforms marked with dot symbols: one, two or three dots. Chain flings from platform to platform toward the button outside the turret room. This is Part 1, and Radio 18A can be missed here.
  2. Look for the side room after the first gap. Radio 18A is in it.
  3. Crouch to get in. The room holds miscellaneous objects and radio 18A, and there is not much to read there. †

Tip: The chamber autosaves on entry.

  1. Press the pedestal button, then crouch under the door piston. That lets you enter the turret room without immediate fire.
  2. Cross the turret room. This is Part 2. Watch for turrets and energy pellets. Retrieve a cube and place it on a button. Then ride the moving platform across, or fling across the angled door panel.
  3. Grab Radio 18B in the same room. It sits on top of the box that holds the pellet receiver; reach it with a floor-portal fling. The camera by the turret-room window can be missed here too.
  4. Enter the final room. This is Part 3: a sequence of linked flings between higher platforms to the exit. Radio 18C is at the bottom of the deep pit that starts the fling in this room. Its signal spot is the middle of the exit platform.
  5. Take the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 19. The ride is one-way.

The elevator and the grill make this a story gate from Test Chamber 18 on to Test Chamber 19.

† Only one source reports this.
