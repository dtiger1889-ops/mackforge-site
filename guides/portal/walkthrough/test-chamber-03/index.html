Portal walkthrough · Part 2: Blue-portal segment

Test Chamber 03

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 03 has no cubes and no buttons. It is pure portal traversal across three piers. Your goal is to use the permanent orange portal on the middle pier to chain blue portals and reach the third pier and the chamberlock. The game saved when the elevator brought you in from Test Chamber 02, and you are still on the same map, testchmb_a_01.

Collectibles here:

  • Three cameras: next to the chamber number sign, above the orange portal, and on the wall before the chamberlock.
  • One radio.

Tip: The orange portal is already waiting for you on the middle pier. You only ever place blue portals.

Walkthrough

  1. You start on the first of three piers. The chamberlock sits on the third. There are no doors, buttons or cubes.
  2. Look for the camera mounted next to the chamber number sign and take it.
  3. Fire a blue portal beside the start and walk through it. A fixed orange portal on the middle pier brings you out there.
  4. From the middle pier, fire a blue portal at a wall on the third pier, near the chamberlock. Then walk back through the permanent orange portal. With the blue end moved, it now brings you out on the third pier.
  5. Detach the camera by the exit. The radio comes with it, routed through the orange portal.
  6. Take the last camera from the wall before the chamberlock.
  7. Exit. Ride the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 04. The trip is one-way, and you cannot come back.

The way out of Test Chamber 03 leads on to Test Chamber 04 and is a story gate: an elevator and a grill, once the chamber is solved.
