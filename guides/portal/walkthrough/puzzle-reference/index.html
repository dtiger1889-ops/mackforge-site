Portal walkthrough · Appendices

Puzzle reference

Spoilers. This page shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Portal is a puzzle game first: you solve it with where you put portals and how you use physics, and each test chamber is a puzzle of its own. These are the kinds of puzzle you’ll meet, and where each one first shows up.

Puzzle type How it works First appears
Portal placement Place portals to make a path across Test Chamber 02, when you get the blue gun
Button activation Move a cube onto a button and keep it held to work a mechanism Test Chamber 00
Height gain Use portals to reach raised surfaces The early chambers
Fling and momentum A floor portal and a wall portal turn a fall into sideways speed Test Chamber 10, where the lessons start; with both portals from Test Chamber 11
Energy-pellet redirect Send the High Energy Pellet through portals into a receiver Test Chamber 06
Turret avoidance and redirect Get around Sentry Turrets, or move them with portals Test Chamber 16 onward
