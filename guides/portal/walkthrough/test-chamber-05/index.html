Portal walkthrough · Part 2: Blue-portal segment

Test Chamber 05

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 05 gives you two cubes and two buttons, so both buttons must be held at the same time to open the exit door. That sets it apart from Test Chamber 04, which has one cube and one button. † The game autosaved when the elevator brought you in from Test Chamber 04, within the same map, testchmb_a_02. The last stretch is new: a permanent orange portal in the ceiling.

Collectibles here:

  • Three cameras: above the doorway next to the chamber number, above or beside the first exit door, and in the final ceiling-portal section. †
  • One radio, behind the two-button door. †

Tip: One cube lies in a pit, just as in Test Chamber 04. Fire a blue portal into the pit to bring it up. †

Walkthrough

  1. Fire a blue portal into the pit and retrieve the cube that lies there. †
  2. Place that first cube on one of the two floor buttons. †
  3. Find the second cube. It sits on one of two raised platforms. The other platform holds a fixed orange portal that you use to reach it. †
  4. Place the second cube on the second button. Both buttons now open the door. †
  5. Before you step through that door, fire a portal under the radio behind it, because the door shuts behind you. Then drop the radio into the pit that held a cube. The pit is where the radio is delivered. †
  6. GLaDOS admits that her earlier claim of no monitoring was not true. It was an outright fabrication. †
  7. Once the door is open, fire a blue portal on the wall beside you and walk through it. A permanent orange portal sits in the ceiling, a floor above the chamberlock, and you fall out of it down next to the chamberlock.
  8. Check for the third camera in this final section, where the ceiling portal drops you. This route is one-way. †
  9. Exit. Ride the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 06. The ride is one-way. †

The way out of Test Chamber 05 leads on to Test Chamber 06 and is a story gate: an elevator and a grill, once the chamber is solved.

The ceiling portal and the camera count of 3 rest on one source only, so treat them with care. †

† Only one source reports this.
