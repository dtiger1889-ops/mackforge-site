Portal walkthrough · Part 3: Dual-portal segment

Test Chamber 11

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Part 3: Dual-portal segment

Test Chamber 11 is where you get the dual portal gun. From gate 5 onward you can fire both orange and blue portals independently. You arrive from Test Chamber 10 by chamberlock elevator, and the game autosaves as the map loads (testchmb_a_07, shared with Test Chamber 12). The floor is toxic goo, so stay on raised surfaces and platforms.

Collectibles here:

  • Camera (1): above the entranceway. Walk into the initial room and turn around; take it before you cross the goo floor.
  • Radio (1): in the main room, on a ledge near the pellet receiver and just in front of the door to the final room. Put a portal next to it and grab it from between the two portals. Carry it to the pedestal where you get the dual portal gun.
  • Achievement “Lab Rat”: it triggers on the dual gun pickup at gate 5, not on the blue-only pickup in Test Chamber 02.

Walkthrough

  1. Listen as GLaDOS warns that the floor will kill; toxic goo covers the main floor.
  2. In the first side room, a door opens briefly across the room. Place a portal through it before it closes.
  3. In the pellet side room, route the pellet (scorch mark portal) into the receiver to start the main moving platform.
  4. Board the moving platform and ride it to the central pedestal. Pick up the fully powered dual (orange and blue) portal device, a key item.
  5. Hear GLaDOS say the device is now more valuable than the combined organs and incomes of everyone in <subject hometown here>.
  6. Know that from gate 5 onward you must create both portal ends, and no static orange portals appear in later chambers.
  7. Exit. Leave Test Chamber 11 by the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 12; the ride is one-way.
