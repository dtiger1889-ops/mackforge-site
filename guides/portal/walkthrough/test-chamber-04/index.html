Portal walkthrough · Part 2: Blue-portal segment

Test Chamber 04

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 04 has one cube and one button. That sets it apart from the two-cube, two-button layout of Test Chamber 05. The cube starts in a pit, and you fetch it with a portal. You come in from Test Chamber 03 by chamberlock elevator. The game autosaved on map load, on testchmb_a_02, a map shared with Test Chamber 05. Map testchmb_a_02 has no autosave in the middle of the chamber, and Test Chamber 05 shares it.

Collectibles here:

  • One radio. It drops with the cube from the dispenser into the pit, so grab it when you retrieve the cube.
  • 2 cameras: one above the button and one on the wall beside the exit. Take both before the emancipation grill.

Tip: The cube is in the pit, but the permanent orange portal is up in the main area near the button, not in the pit. You need a blue portal down in the pit to get back up.

Walkthrough

  1. Watch the dispenser drop a cube into the pit. The radio falls with it.
  2. Drop down into the pit. Fire a blue portal on a pit wall and step through it.
  3. You come out of the permanent orange portal in the main area near the button, and you bring the cube with you. Pick up the radio at the same time.
  4. Place the cube on the floor button. This opens the exit door.
  5. Grab the 2 cameras, above the button and on the wall beside the exit, before the emancipation grill.
  6. As you leave, GLaDOS says the next chamber will be unmonitored. You cannot hear this line again.
  7. Exit. Take the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 05. The ride is one-way.

The way out of Test Chamber 04 leads on to Test Chamber 05 and is a story gate: an elevator and a grill, once the chamber is solved.
