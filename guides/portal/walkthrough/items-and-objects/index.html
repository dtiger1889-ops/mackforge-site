Portal walkthrough · Appendices

Items and objects

Spoilers. This page shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Portal has a minimal item set. There is no inventory, no drops, and no persistent carrying of items between chambers. Items here are the one tool and the notable objects you interact with.

The portal gun

Portal’s sole interactive tool. It is not a combat weapon, but it is the only item occupying the “weapon/tool” slot. The physics underneath it live in How the game works.

The Aperture Science Handheld Portal Device (the “portal gun”) fires two linked portals, blue and orange. Only one of each colour exists at a time; firing a new blue portal moves the existing blue one.

You get it in two stages. The blue-only version comes in Test Chamber 02; the dual-portal (orange) version comes in Test Chamber 11.

The blue-only device is retrieved from a pit in Test Chamber 02 (via automated portals). The fully powered dual-portal device is acquired in Test Chamber 11, and picking up the orange portion triggers the “Lab Rat” achievement. Test Chamber 00 and Test Chamber 01 have no player-held gun.

A portal attaches only to flat, light-coloured, concrete-type surfaces. Dark metallic brown or black panels, glass, and moving surfaces reject portals (see How the game works).

The gun passes freely through Material Emancipation Grills, but doing so resets (removes) both active portals and shakes the gun.

Things you collect

Portal counts two finite sets toward achievements: cameras you detach from walls (Camera Shy) and radios you carry to a signal spot (Transmission Received). Each row below feeds a collection achievement. The achievement entries are in Achievements, and the missable summary is in Missables.

Security cameras (33): Camera Shy needs all 33 detached. Skipping or replaying a chamber resets the counter, so get every camera the first time through each chamber.

Hidden radios (26): only the Test Chamber 00 radio exists on a first run. The other 25 appear once the game has been completed, and carrying them to their signal spots earns Transmission Received.

Hidden radios

Radios other than the Test Chamber 00 one do not exist until the game is beaten once. Do a dedicated post-completion pass. †

Carry each radio to its signal spot; the light turns green when it is placed correctly. Radios are added to existing chambers (not a separate area). Apart from the Test Chamber 00 radio, they only appear after the game is completed once. †
