About these projects

I've always tried to streamline anything I have to do more than once. I'm not a developer, but coding agents gave me a way to turn that habit into actual tools. Most of what you'll find here started with something in my own life that was slow, annoying, or too easy to forget, and then I kept working on it long after the sensible stopping point.
