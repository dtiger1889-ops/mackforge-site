Portal walkthrough · Part 2: Blue-portal segment

Test Chamber 02

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Part 2: Blue-portal segment

Test Chamber 02 is where you get your portal gun, though for now it only fires blue portals. The game saved as this map loaded on your way in from Test Chamber 01 (testchmb_a_01, which also holds Test Chamber 03).

Collectibles here:

  • Cameras (3): above the main observation window, at the end of the stairway, and in the entranceway. Detach them all before you pass the Emancipation Grill at the exit.
  • Radio (1): on the wall section with a hole in it. Put a portal on the ceiling above it to reach it.

Every camera and radio in the game is listed in Items and objects.

Walkthrough

  1. You arrive on a ledge behind a window that looks over the whole chamber. Below, a portal gun spins on a central stand, firing portals by itself.
  2. Go down the stairway; a portal appears, and walking through it puts you on the opposite ledge.
  3. Step onto the spinning stand to pick up the gun. It fires blue portals only, and an orange portal stays fixed on the ledge.
  4. Fire a blue portal onto a wall you can reach and walk through to get back to the entry ledge. The chamberlock door is open now; it opened by itself.
  5. Exit. Test Chamber 02 ends at the chamberlock elevator, and the ride to Test Chamber 03 is one-way.

Tip: Picking up this blue-only gun does not unlock the Lab Rat achievement. That comes in Test Chamber 11, when you get the fully powered two-portal device.
