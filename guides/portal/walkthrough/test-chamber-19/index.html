Portal walkthrough · Part 3: Dual-portal segment

Test Chamber 19

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

This is the last test chamber, and it ends with your escape from the fire pit. It is the one with the permanent green pellet, a distinctive green glow found only here.

You arrive from Test Chamber 18 via chamberlock elevator. The map load autosaves (testchmb_a_15, which also holds the first stretch of the escape, Escape (Part 1)).

A brief “Loading” message comes later, before the five-piston area. †

Collectibles here:

  • 3 cameras (first room, annex, end of the platform hallway) and 1 radio (in the fire pit). The cameras are all collected before the fire pit; fetch the radio with a portal once you are on the platform above the pit.
  • Cameras: right wall of the first room; above the small button in the annex; at the end of the hallway with the permanent pellet. You can miss all 3 of them. †

Tip: Collect everything before you reach the oven. After gate 7 you leave the test chambers permanently.

Walkthrough

  1. Start in the first room. It has a pellet launcher, a receiver, and two platforms angled at 45 degrees. Route the pellet into the receiver to start the moving platforms. The permanent (green) pellet is not in this room; it is in the hallway you ride through on the platform. A camera here can be missed. †
  2. Listen to the “promised cake” announcement. GLaDOS frames the final test and the equipment recovery annex (“both hands empty before any cake”). †
  3. Ride the moving platform to the side room (the annex) with a button. The button opens a nearby door only briefly. Put a portal on the wall near the button and press it. Put the other portal on the wall behind the door that opened. Step through onto the platform as it passes. †
  4. Ride on through the hallway with the permanent green pellet. A wall sits in its path. The platform carries you straight to the fire pit, the “party escort” oven. †
  5. Grab the last camera at the end of the platform hallway, before the fire pit. There are none after it, and gate 7 is the point of no return. “Partygoer” is unavoidable if you survive the oven section. †

Tip: Gate 7 is the Camera Shy point of no return: there are no cameras in Escape Sequence / Surface Finale, so collect all Test Chamber 19 cameras (gates 01-03) before the fire pit (gate 06). †

  1. Spot the radio in the giant incinerator (the fire pit), against a wall, from the balcony above. Put a portal near you and another behind the radio, and grab it partway through the portal. Carry it on through the side rooms. Its signal spot is the center of the side office after the two big fans. †
  2. Do not ride into the fire. Instead, portal up to the platform above the oven. This earns “Partygoer: Make the correct party escort submission position decision” and begins the escape. †
  3. Go on through the side rooms (collapsing stairs, locked mesh door), the two big fans, the poison-pit walkway and the cube tube. The tube drops you into the Test Chamber 01 room. Stand on the big button; the chamberlock malfunctions and you jump down the elevator shaft. The maintenance areas of the escape continue from there. †

Tip: The drop down the elevator shaft, back in the Test Chamber 01 room, is scripted, not a puzzle failure.

  1. Take the oven escape on to Escape Sequence / Surface Finale. This is a permanent point of no return. There is no way back to the test chambers after this point.

The way on from Test Chamber 19 to Escape Sequence / Surface Finale is permanent (Partygoer): escape the fire pit.

† Only one source reports this.
