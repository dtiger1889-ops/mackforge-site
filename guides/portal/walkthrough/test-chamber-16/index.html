Portal walkthrough · Part 3: Dual-portal segment

Test Chamber 16

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 16 brings the first new enemy type: turrets. It is the last-but-one turret chamber, and GLaDOS calls it a “live-fire course for military androids.” Turrets are explained in How the game works.

Collectibles here:

  • Five cameras and one radio. The radio is in the side room with the stacked cubes, past the two side-by-side turrets. Its signal spot is the hidden crawlspace behind the extended panel, next to the writing. The radio signal only activates once you have completed the game (Transmission Received).
  • Cameras (5): above the window across from the entrance; after the first turret around the corner; behind the third turret in the alcove; in the button room across from the door; and after the first fence. †

Tip: Turrets fire on line-of-sight, and bullet-proof glass blocks their shots. Use portals, or the cubes from the cube dispenser, as shields to neutralize them.

Walkthrough

  1. You arrive from Test Chamber 15 by chamberlock elevator. The game autosaves when the map testchmb_a_11 loads, and that map holds Chamber 16 only.
  2. The first turret faces away from the entrance. Disarm it by portal-drop or pickup.
  3. Three turrets fire through the entry glass. The glass is bullet-proof, so stay behind it.
  4. In the side room, a malfunctioning cube dispenser sends out many cubes you can use as shields against turret fire.
  5. In the button room, three turrets are marked with a red X. Disable them and place a cube on the button to open the door.
  6. Knock one turret over with another to earn “Friendly Fire”, which is “Knock down a turret with another turret.” This is an early, turret-dense place to earn it, and it is not lost by leaving this chamber, since turrets also appear later, in Test Chamber 18 and in the escape.
  7. The exit is blocked by fencing and turrets. Shoot portals through the fences to disable them.
  8. Ride the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 17. This ride is one-way.

Tip: Friendly Fire has an opportunity at gate 5, knocking one turret over with another. This is the most reliable spot; turrets also appear later, in Test Chamber 18 and in the escape.

From Test Chamber 16 the only way on is Test Chamber 17, through the elevator and the grill, once you solve the chamber.

The radio signal in Test Chamber 16 activates only on a second playthrough, since Transmission Received is post-completion. Friendly Fire is not lost by leaving this chamber, because turrets also appear in Test Chamber 18 and in the escape.

† Only one source reports this.
