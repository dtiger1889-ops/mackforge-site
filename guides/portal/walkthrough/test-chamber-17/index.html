Portal walkthrough · Part 3: Dual-portal segment

Test Chamber 17

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 17 is a story chamber built around the Weighted Companion Cube. † The exit stays locked until the cube is incinerated, and that choice is permanent within the run. It earns the Fratricide achievement.

Collectibles here:

  • Radio (1): beneath the third platform. Collect it after gate 4 raises the platforms. †
  • Cameras (2), both detachable: one next to where the second pellet hits the wall, and one above the doorway to the small pellet-launcher room. Other cameras in the chamber sit on walls that take no portals and do not count toward Camera Shy.
  • Lore: a hidden den with murals and writings behind the ground-level extended panel. Skip the panel and you miss it. †

Tip: The Companion Cube is a tool. Use it as a step and as a pellet shield in the corridors. The exit does not open until it is incinerated at gate 8.

Walkthrough

  1. You arrive from Test Chamber 16 by chamberlock elevator. The game autosaves when the map testchmb_a_13 loads, and that map holds Chamber 17 only.
  2. The Vital Apparatus Vent delivers the Weighted Companion Cube, marked with a heart. GLaDOS narrates its care. †
  3. Use the cube as a step to climb the high ledges to the platform area. †
  4. Cross the two corridors of bouncing pellets while you hold the cube as a shield. †
  5. In the three-platform area, route pellets into three receivers to raise three platforms, then cross with the cube. †
  6. Optional: behind the ground-level extended panel is a hidden den with murals and writings, reachable only through portals. This is the second Rattman den. Cameras stacked from it reach the raised platform that holds the Companion Cube without routing every pellet. †
  7. Once the platforms are up, take the radio from beneath the third platform. †
  8. In the final side room, two doors powered by two buttons guard a receiver. Use the cube on the raised button. †
  9. Press the pedestal to open the incinerator aperture, then drop the cube in. This is the Emergency Intelligence Incinerator, and it earns “Fratricide”, which is “Do whatever it takes to survive.” The step is one-way. †
  10. Ride the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 18. This ride is one-way. †

Gate 8 is a hard lock inside the chamber: the exit will not open until the cube is dropped into the incinerator. The Rattman den at gate 5 is optional lore, and you miss it if you skip the extended panel. Fratricide is unavoidable on a normal playthrough. †

From Test Chamber 17 the only way on is Test Chamber 18, through the elevator after the incinerator. The key is the Companion Cube incinerated, and the lock is permanent within the run.

† Only one source reports this.
