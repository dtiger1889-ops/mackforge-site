Developer console

Turn it on at Options, then Keyboard, then Advanced, then Enable developer console. Open it with ~.

Command What it does
sv_cheats 1 Enables cheats. It disables achievement unlocks and challenge-medal submission for the session, so earn challenge medals legitimately.†
cl_showpos 1 On-screen position and velocity readout, used by speedrunners.†
cl_drawhud 0/1 Toggles the HUD.†
sv_portal_placement_never_fail 1 Forces portal placements to succeed.†
portals_resizeall <w> <h> Resizes all portals.†
sv_gravity <value> Adjusts gravity (default 600).†
host_timescale <value> Speeds up or slows down game time.†
fov_desired <value> Field of view beyond the menu slider. The slider covers 75 to 110, and fov_desired goes up to 120.†
fov / cl_fov <value> Field of view. The sources checked do not confirm whether these need sv_cheats.†
sensitivity <value> Mouse sensitivity.†

Controller

A controller is supported. Full controller support has been native since the 2022 update, and StrategyWiki’s Controls page has Xbox 360, PlayStation 3 and Nintendo Switch columns. The exact button mapping is not recorded here.

Technique notes

Mouse sensitivity. It matters for portal-aiming precision. The speedrun Glitchless category caps sensitivity at 10.0. Tune it for accurate edge placements, which matter for Camera Shy (Missables).†

Fling. Falling into a floor portal and exiting a wall portal converts fall speed to horizontal velocity. The full mechanic is in How the game works.

Crosshair colour. The crosshair changes colour over a valid portal surface. Learn to read it for fast orientation and edge placements.

† Only one source reports this.
