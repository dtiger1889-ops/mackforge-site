Portal walkthrough · Part 2: Blue-portal segment

Test Chamber 06

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 06 is the first High Energy Pellet chamber. It introduces the bouncing energy pellet, its launcher and its receiver, and the brown walls that your portals cannot use. The layout is the simplest of its kind: a single pellet that powers a fixed lift. The game autosaved on map load when you arrived from Test Chamber 05, on testchmb_a_03, a map shared with Test Chamber 07.

Collectibles here:

  • One radio, above the pellet launcher on the corner nearest the entrance, on the edge of the ceiling piece the launcher hangs from.
  • Radio 7/26 is this one (see Items and objects).
  • There are no cameras here.

Tip: Your goal is to get the pellet from its launcher into the receiver, which powers the lift. Look at the red light the receiver casts on the ceiling.

Walkthrough

  1. Keep your distance from the pellet. It kills on contact, so stay clear of its path until you understand the routing.
  2. Look at the walls. Some are dark brown and cannot take a portal, while the light-grey panels are portalable.
  3. Find the pellet launcher. A fixed orange portal sits beneath it, and the pellet it fires kills instantly on contact.
  4. Find the pellet receiver by the red light it casts on the ceiling. That light marks where a portal needs to go.
  5. Place a blue portal across from the receiver so the pellet exits aimed at it. Use the red ceiling light as your aiming guide.
  6. Pellets recharge their lifespan each time they pass through a portal, which gives you more time before they disintegrate.
  7. Before you leave, get the radio above the launcher. Fire a portal on the ceiling near it, grab the radio through the portal, and carry it to the victory lift.
  8. Ride the victory lift to the chamberlock. The captured pellet powers it, and the lift is one-way.
  9. Exit. Take the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 07. The ride is one-way.

The way out of Test Chamber 06 leads on to Test Chamber 07 and is a story gate: an elevator and a grill, once the chamber is solved.
