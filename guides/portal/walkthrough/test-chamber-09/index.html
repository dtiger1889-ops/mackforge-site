Portal walkthrough · Part 2: Blue-portal segment

Test Chamber 09

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

In Test Chamber 09, GLaDOS declares the test impossible and tells you not to attempt it. What is new is the emancipation grid: here it is a puzzle element, not just an exit device. Look around the main room of Test Chamber 09, because it is revisited, rearranged, during the escape sequence. You arrive from Test Chamber 08 by chamberlock elevator, and the game autosaves as the map loads (testchmb_a_05, which holds Chamber 09 only).

Collectibles here:

  • Radio (1): wedged in the gap between the two sections. It is risky to grab, since it can be destroyed in the emancipation grid, so quicksave with F6 first.
  • No cameras in this chamber.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the cube up to a platform through the fixed orange portal.
  2. Remember that an emancipation grid separates the two sections, and destroyed cubes are re-dispensed. The grid in the middle of the room destroys the cube.
  3. If you lose the cube, approach the dispenser platform again to receive a replacement.
  4. Pass a cube through to the button section and place it on the button to open the chamberlock door.
  5. Exit. Leave Test Chamber 09 by the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 10; the ride is one-way.
