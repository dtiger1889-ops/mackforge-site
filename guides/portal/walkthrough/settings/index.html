Portal walkthrough · Appendices

Settings

Spoilers. This page shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

The options that help with portal puzzles. The tab grouping below is approximate, and commands for the rest are in Controls.

Video

Setting Menu path (approx.) Effect on play
Field of View (FOV) Options, then Video The default is narrow, about 75. The slider covers 75 to 110, and topped out at 90 before the 2022 patch. Raising it widens your awareness, which helps with flings and spotting portal surfaces. To go past the slider, fov_desired reaches 120.†
Motion blur Options, then Video Disable it (recommended). It reduces visual clarity during fast movement and flings.†
Vsync Options, then Video Disable it if you notice input lag during fast portal traversal.†
Resolution Options, then Video Higher resolution sharpens portal-edge clarity, which helps edge placements and Camera Shy.†

Keyboard and mouse

Mouse sensitivity affects portal-aiming precision; see Controls.†

The developer console is enabled from Options, then Keyboard, then Advanced, then Enable developer console.†

Accessibility

Portal predates most modern accessibility menus. Community workarounds run through console commands, such as FOV widening and crosshair or HUD toggles. No dedicated accessibility tab is documented. The Audio settings do include subtitles and closed captions.†

† Only one source reports this.

† Sources rate this as medium confidence: likely right, not fully confirmed.
