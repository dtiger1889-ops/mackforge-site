Portal walkthrough · Part 3: Dual-portal segment

Test Chamber 13

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 13 is the first of six chambers that come back after you finish the game as Advanced and Challenge versions. † The puzzle here is a two-button problem: both buttons must be held at once, using a cube and the pellet’s moving platform. †

Collectibles here:

  • Cameras (3): above the main entranceway, on the main-room wall beside the entrance, and on the final-passage wall. †
  • Radio (1): on a small ledge in the main room that the moving platform passes. Grab it from the platform. †

Tip: Start gently. The pellet starts a moving platform, and a cube plus the ceiling symbols tell you where the two buttons get their weight. †

Walkthrough

  1. You arrive from Test Chamber 12 by chamberlock elevator. The game autosaves when the map testchmb_a_08 loads, and that map holds Chamber 13 only.
  2. In the first room, grab a cube and reach the door using two created portals. †
  3. Portal the pellet from the scorch-mark wall to the ceiling red-spot and into the receiver. That starts a moving platform. †
  4. Pull the cube off the platform and set it on the higher button. Put a portal on the one-dot ceiling symbol above that button, carry the cube through and set it down. †
  5. Fetch the second cube from the first room’s button. Portals connecting the two rooms let you carry it through. Put it on the other button using the two-dot ceiling symbol, or stand on that button yourself. With both buttons held, the exit door opens. †
  6. Step on the exit button, fire a portal through the opened door, drop through, and reach the chamberlock. The elevator there goes to Test Chamber 14. †
  7. Take the cameras and the radio on the way. Bring the radio back to the first room, near the window above the button. †
  8. Ride the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 14. This ride is one-way.

From Test Chamber 13 the only way on is Test Chamber 14, through the elevator and the grill. You solve the chamber to get there.

† Only one source reports this.
