Portal walkthrough · Part 4: Escape Sequence

Escape Sequence / Surface Finale

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Part 4: Escape Sequence

After Test Chamber 19, you leave the test chambers behind. You make your way through the behind-the-scenes maintenance areas, guided by Doug Rattmann’s red graffiti, and then you confront GLaDOS.

You come from Test Chamber 19 (the fire-pit escape, gate 7). Chamber 19 and Escape (Part 1) share one map (testchmb_a_15).

A brief “Loading” message comes before the five-piston area. †

Rusty maintenance tunnels with Rattman’s red directional arrows point the route, so follow the arrows.

Collectibles here:

  • Three radios (24, 25, 26) are spread across the escape maps, and all are collectible. There are no cameras: the Camera Shy set ends at Test Chamber 19.
  • Radios 24, 25 and 26 are in the escape maps. Radio 26 is in the final large turret room with the muddy trench; bring it to the two large electrical engines or generators near the top. See Items and objects.

Tip: The “Long Jump” achievement (travel 300 feet in a single fling) is best attempted in the large room with the muddy trench early in the escape.

Tip: Long Jump is most easily earned in this section’s large room with the muddy trench (see Achievements).

Walkthrough

  1. Follow the rusty back stairways and tunnels marked with Rattman writings and red arrows pointing the route. You cannot go back through them. †
  2. Reach the room of five large vertical pistons and portal-stand past them. Radio 24 is in a side room, reached by a portal through a gate before this room. You can miss it. †
  3. In the ceiling-piston room, the pistons strike the ceiling. Ride a piston and double-fling up to the next landing. †
  4. Walk along the cube-transport tubes over goo, ride a piston, and reach a door that will not open and a hole in the floor. Drop down the hole; a brief “Loading” message follows. †
  5. Enter the turret ambush room. Three doors open in sequence, each revealing a turret. Disable each one quickly. Radio 25 is on a table in the malfunctioning turret room, and you can miss it. †
  6. Face the rocket turret, which cannot be destroyed. Trick its rockets into breaking a storage-cube tube and freeing a cube. †
  7. Portal through a fan and across the goo and sewers toward GLaDOS’s chamber (escape_01 then escape_02). †
  8. Cross the non-lethal goo with its green generators; the radio is in the goo, and you can miss Radio 26. Then fling up to the suspended walkway leading to GLaDOS. †

Tip: The game autosaves on each escape map load (escape_00, escape_01, escape_02), and a designer-placed autosave may fall before the boss arena. †

Tip: escape_00, escape_01 and escape_02 each save on load, and a possible designer-placed autosave comes before the boss arena. The three radios across the escape are 24, 25 and 26. No cameras exist: the Camera Shy count ends at Test Chamber 19. All escape gates are one-way. †

The GLaDOS fight

  1. Shortly after you enter, GLaDOS drops a Morality Core (purple). Incinerate it: press the red button and drop the core through a portal into the incinerator. This starts a 6-minute neurotoxin countdown and activates a rocket sentry (the rocket turret).
  2. Carry the morality core to the incinerator, press the switch, and incinerate it. This triggers the neurotoxin countdown. †
  3. Redirect the rocket turret’s rockets through portals into GLaDOS to dislodge each of her remaining cores. Incinerate all four. †
  4. This is the intended defeat method: redirect the rocket turret’s rockets through a portal into GLaDOS to dislodge each remaining core. Core locations are fixed each playthrough: one lands on a light fixture, one on a ledge up above, and one is held in mid-air. Incinerate all of them within the time limit. †

Tip: There is a faster exploit. A single live turret’s bullets also damage GLaDOS. A short spray can dislodge multiple cores at once, without waiting for each to incinerate, much faster than the rocket method. †

  1. Destroy the final core. That ends the fight and triggers “Heartbreaker.”
  2. Watch the ending. After the final core, the boss sequence concludes and the game completes, earning “Heartbreaker: Complete Portal” (credits and “Still Alive”). †
  3. The implosion ejects Chell to the surface. After the credits, the camera reveals the cake. The credits song “Still Alive” was composed and arranged by Jonathan Coulton and performed by Ellen McLain (GLaDOS’ voice and the in-game singer).
  4. This is the exit, and it is permanent: defeating GLaDOS leads to the end and credits, and the game ends.

To get there, destroy all 4 personality cores. The way on is permanent.
