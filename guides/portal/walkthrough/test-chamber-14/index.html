Portal walkthrough · Part 3: Dual-portal segment

Test Chamber 14

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 14 is a fling-and-momentum challenge that mixes cubes, pellets, vertical scaffolds and goo. The layout has two parts: a first room with stairs, a raised cube and a button, which opens into a sludge room with sinking platforms. The pellet antechamber beyond that ends at the victory lift.

Collectibles here:

  • Radio (1): on a ledge on the right, between the button and the door it powers, on the right wall. It is also described as a ledge to the right of the hallway you arrive from. The radio that sits on the extended platform support belongs to Test Chamber 15.
  • This is radio 15/26 of the whole game; every radio is listed in Items and objects.
  • No cameras in this chamber.
  • No detachable cameras either.

Tip: This chamber looks like it wants you to cross several rooms, but there is a faster route straight up out of the first room.

Walkthrough

  1. You arrive from Test Chamber 13 by chamberlock elevator. The game autosaves when the map testchmb_a_09 loads, and that map holds Chamber 14 only.
  2. Walk forward. Stairs rise to the pellet-receiver area, and a cube sits on an unclimbable raised platform that you reach by a fling.
  3. Place the cube on the button to open the door to the sludge room.
  4. Grab the radio from the ledge on the right: use momentum and a portal below it as you fall past. Then bring it to the stationary platform in the middle of the sludge.
  5. Cross the goo-filled sludge room on its platforms, which rise and sink, to reach the pellet-launcher antechamber.
  6. Place a portal on the scorch mark and another above the receiver to route the pellet around corners.
  7. The captured pellet activates the victory lift. Ride it to the chamberlock, then take the elevator to Test Chamber 15. This ride is one-way.

Tip: The sludge platforms are hazardous but not instantly lethal. The rising and retracting stairs and the long pellet routing are what make this layout distinctive.

The shortcut. The intended long route works, but a single fling from the pit left by the retracted stairs can land you straight on the raised Victory Lift. Without leaving the first room, put a portal on the ground in front of the Victory Lift, as close to it as you can, and the other at the bottom of the pit left by the retracted stairs. Jump in and steer your flight to land on the raised lift, finishing the chamber early. †

The only way on from Test Chamber 14 is Test Chamber 15, through the elevator and the grill, once you route the pellet into the receiver.

The Terminal Velocity achievement, the infinite fall, works in any chamber with portal surfaces on both the ceiling and the floor; see Achievements.

† Only one source reports this.
