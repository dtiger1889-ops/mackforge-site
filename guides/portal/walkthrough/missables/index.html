Portal walkthrough · Appendices

Missables

Spoilers. This page shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

What you can lose, and when you lose it. Check here before you leave a chamber.

Lost for the rest of the run

Security cameras (33), for Camera Shy. They are in Test Chamber 02, Test Chamber 03, Test Chamber 04, Test Chamber 05, Test Chamber 10, Test Chamber 11, Test Chamber 13, Test Chamber 15, Test Chamber 16, Test Chamber 17, Test Chamber 18 and Test Chamber 19. The latest camera is in Test Chamber 19, and there are no cameras after it. Get every camera the first time through each chamber, because skipping or replaying a chamber resets the counter. A quick check: when you leave Test Chamber 02 your count should read 3. If a count is ever low, restart. Per-camera locations are in Items and objects.

Easy to lose on the spot

The radio in Test Chamber 05. Fire a portal under the radio before you step through the two-button door, because the door shuts behind you. Then drop the radio into the pit that held a cube.

Radios over goo or acid, in Test Chamber 15 and Test Chamber 18. Quicksave before you fling a radio across goo. Losing one can force a chamber replay.

Locked until you finish once

Hidden radios (26), for Transmission Received. They are in Test Chamber 00 to Test Chamber 19 and the escape. Only the radio in Test Chamber 00 exists on a first run. The other radios appear after the game is completed once, so collect them on a dedicated post-completion run. Locations are in Items and objects.

A myth

Many people say that interrupting GLaDOS’s camera voice lines breaks Camera Shy. That is false: the camera-detach counter and the voice-line counter are separate, and only the detach counter feeds the achievement.†

† Sources rate this as medium confidence: likely right, not fully confirmed.
