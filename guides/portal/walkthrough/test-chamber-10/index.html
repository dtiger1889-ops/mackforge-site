Portal walkthrough · Part 2: Blue-portal segment

Test Chamber 10

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 10 is the first fling and momentum tutorial chamber, and floor diagrams illustrate the mechanic. The goal is to chain three momentum rooms from entry to chamberlock: downward momentum converts to horizontal, then a double fling through the same floor portal carries you up. You arrive from Test Chamber 09 by chamberlock elevator, and the game autosaves as the map loads (testchmb_a_06, which holds Chamber 10 only).

Collectibles here:

  • Camera (1): in the upper corner of the first room, the chamber’s most distinctive landmark.
  • Radio (1): in the third section, grabbed mid-climb on the middle level.

Tip: Study the floor diagrams in the first room before you go on; they explain the momentum mechanic.

Walkthrough

  1. In the first room, spot the step that is too high to jump. A fixed orange portal sits on a suspended panel above it.
  2. Fire a blue portal in a wall and walk in to gain momentum onto the step.
  3. Grab the camera from the upper corner of this room if you want it.
  4. In the second room, follow the floor diagrams: downward momentum converts to horizontal. Drop into a floor portal to cross the pit.
  5. In the third room, look up and the wall panels slide out. Put a blue portal as high as you can on an extended panel and fall into the fixed orange portal in the pit floor.
  6. The panels move again. Put a second blue portal on a panel and fall through the same orange portal a second time to reach the upper level.
  7. Grab the radio from the third section, mid-climb on the middle level.
  8. Exit. Leave Test Chamber 10 by the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 11; the ride is one-way.
