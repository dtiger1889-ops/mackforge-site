Portal walkthrough · Part 2: Blue-portal segment

Test Chamber 08

Spoilers. This chapter shows everything in it, puzzle solutions included, and sometimes points ahead to later chambers.

Test Chamber 08 puts acid under everything: the whole floor is lethal. The pellet routing is harder too, because the pellet has to bounce off a wall before it reaches the receiver, a more complex route than in Test Chamber 06/07. You arrive from Test Chamber 07 by chamberlock elevator, and the game autosaves as the map loads (testchmb_a_04, which holds Chamber 08 only).

Collectibles here:

  • Radio (1): under the moving platform’s start position. Grab it from the fixed platform that holds the orange portal, not while riding. Put a blue portal behind or near the radio and reach through. Then bring it back to the entrance, near the chamber number.
  • No cameras in this chamber.

Tip: The automatic save happens only at chamber entry, so quicksave with F6 before every hazardous move.

Walkthrough

  1. Listen to GLaDOS announce a “consequence for failure”. She means the floor: any contact with the acid is fatal.
  2. Place a blue portal on the burn mark on the far wall, so the pellet is redirected out of the orange portal in the floor.
  3. Place a second portal to route the redirected pellet into the receiver; that starts the moving platform.
  4. Fetch the radio now if you want it, from the fixed platform with the orange portal.
  5. Portal to the ledge.
  6. Portal beside the platform track, then ride the platform to the chamberlock.
  7. Exit. Leave Test Chamber 08 by the chamberlock elevator to Test Chamber 09; the ride is one-way.
